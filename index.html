Dr. Sayan Shee

Postdoctoral Fellow at the Schulich Faculty of Chemistry, Technion-Israel Institute of Technology. Current research focuses on stereoselective synthesis of complex molecules.

During his Ph.D., he worked on sustainable N-heterocyclic carbene (NHC) organocatalysis.
